Dive in, project opens

Clicking a card plays a 1.5s camera dive toward the globe, then a full-screen in-page panel opens with that property's site loaded inside — no new browser tab. A small bar up top shows the project name and a close control; closing returns to the same Earth screen to pick another.

Then: close the panel, or continue to the VR tour

Session lands in reporting

Ending the session logs total time, which cards were opened and when, the customer, and which staff member ran it. It shows up immediately in the admin dashboard (every session) and that staff member's manager dashboard (their team only) — staff is handed back to lead search, ready for the next customer.
